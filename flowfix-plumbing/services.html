Service 01

Pipe Repair

Leaking, damaged, or noisy pipes need prompt professional attention. We inspect the affected pipework, explain the cause in plain language, and confirm an estimate before starting any repair.

  • Leaking and damaged pipe repair
  • Visible pipe leaks at joints and connections
  • Joint and connection issues
  • General pipe maintenance advice
Book Pipe Repair View related work photo

For safety, we do not recommend DIY repair of live water pipes. Isolate the supply if safe to do so and arrange professional assistance.

Service 02

Drain Unblocking

Slow-draining sinks and blocked bathroom or kitchen drains are among the most common plumbing callouts. We assess the blockage, identify the likely cause, and clear the drain with the appropriate method.

  • Slow-draining sinks and basins
  • Blocked bathroom and shower drains
  • Kitchen drain and waste issues
  • General drain maintenance guidance

Professional inspection may be appropriate if blockages keep returning, multiple drains are affected, or there is a sewage odour — these can indicate deeper line issues.

Service 03

Tap Replacement

A dripping tap wastes water and can damage worktops over time. We repair or replace taps and help you choose fixtures that suit your bathroom or kitchen.

  • Dripping and noisy tap repair
  • Worn washers, cartridges, and seals
  • Tap upgrades and style changes
  • Full replacement and new installation

Service 04

Water Tank Cleaning

Regular tank cleaning supports healthy water storage and plumbing performance. We inspect the tank first, agree a clear scope of work, then complete the cleaning carefully.

  • Cleaning service overview and scope agreement
  • General maintenance checks around the tank
  • Inspection before cleaning begins
  • Clear description of completed work

We do not make water-purity or health guarantees. Cleaning removes sediment and build-up; water quality outcomes depend on many factors outside the cleaning itself.
