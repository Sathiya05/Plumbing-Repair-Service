Our Story

Built Around Dependable Repairs

FlowFix Plumbing Co. was started with a simple idea: plumbing work should be explained honestly, priced clearly, and finished properly. Too many homeowners have experienced vague estimates, unfinished jobs, or rushed visits — we set out to do things differently.

Today, our focus remains the same. We handle pipe repairs, drain unblocking, tap replacement, water tank cleaning, and general plumbing maintenance for residential and small commercial properties. Every visit starts with listening, continues with a clear estimate, and ends with work you can inspect before we leave.

Service Standards

We hold ourselves to practical standards that protect your property and your peace of mind. These are the behaviours you can expect on every visit.

  • Clean working practices — protective coverings where needed and a tidy workspace on completion
  • Clear communication — updates if timings change and explanations in everyday language
  • Quality checks — we test the repair before packing up and invite you to inspect the result
  • Honest recommendations — we suggest only the work that is needed, with alternatives where relevant
