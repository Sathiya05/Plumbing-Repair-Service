Cost Factors

What Affects the Cost?

Every job is different — these are the main variables that shape an estimate.

Type of Problem

A simple joint reseal differs from a full pipe section replacement.

Parts Required

Fixture quality, pipe material, and speciality parts change material costs.

Accessibility

Under-sink, under-floor, or tight-access work can take more time.

Work Complexity

Multiple faults, older systems, or concealed pipework increase complexity.

Emergency Timing

After-hours, weekend, or urgent scheduling may include additional fees.

Scenario A

Simple Tap Drip

A single dripping kitchen tap with a worn washer. Accessible, standard fixture — typically the lower end of the range for tap work.

Factors: one fixture, easy access, basic parts

Scenario B

Leak Behind Tiles

A pipe leak detected behind bathroom tiling. Locating and accessing the pipe adds inspection and labour time before any repair begins.

Factors: concealed pipe, access work, diagnosis

Scenario C

Recurring Kitchen Blockage

A kitchen drain that keeps blocking. Clearing may be straightforward, but a deeper inspection could be recommended to find the root cause.

Factors: severity, possible deeper line issues
