Be Prepared

Before Your Appointment

A little preparation helps us diagnose the issue faster and arrive ready to work.

  • Describe the issue clearly — where it is and what you've observed
  • Share when the problem started — and whether it's getting worse
  • Mention visible leaks or blockages — including any previous repair attempts
  • Keep the affected area accessible if it is safe to do so — move items away from under sinks or around manholes
  • Have your phone nearby in case the technician needs to call on arrival
