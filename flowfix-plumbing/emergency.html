Know the Signs

Common Plumbing Emergencies

The right response depends on the situation — when in doubt, prioritise safety and call for professional help.

Burst or Severely Leaking Pipe

A burst pipe releases water rapidly and can cause significant damage. Shut off the mains supply if safe, then contact us immediately.

Uncontrolled Water Leak

A leak you cannot stop with a valve or bucket may need urgent professional isolation and repair.

Major Drain Blockage

A fully blocked drain causing overflow or backup needs prompt attention to limit damage and hygiene risk.

Sudden Plumbing Failure

Loss of water, overflowing tanks, or other sudden failures that leave your property without safe plumbing use.

Safety First

What to Do First

General safety guidance only — every situation is different. Do not attempt risky repairs yourself.

  • If safe to do so, turn off the relevant water supply valve (or the mains stopcock).
  • Keep away from water near electrical equipment, sockets, and appliances.
  • Do not touch electrical devices in wet areas — switch off power at the breaker only if safe and dry to reach.
  • Contact emergency services (fire, ambulance, utility) if there is immediate danger to people or property.
  • Arrange qualified professional assistance — call FlowFix or another licensed plumber as soon as possible.

We do not encourage DIY repair of gas lines, pressurised pipes, or any situation where you feel unsafe. When in doubt, leave the area and call for help.
